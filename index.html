✦ Chapter One of Forever ✦

How the Sky Learned Your Name

This is not a story about meeting someone. This is a story about the universe finally exhaling — because two stars, separated for lifetimes, had found each other again.

i.

Before the First Word

There is a particular silence that lives between two souls before they know each other. Not empty — waiting. Like a song holding its breath. For years the universe kept us on separate shelves, learning languages, walking different roads.

And then, on an ordinary day that the calendar forgot to mark, our orbits quietly brushed. No thunder. No announcement. Just a sudden, unfamiliar warmth — like standing near a window when the sun finally finds you.

ii.

The Conversation That Wouldn't End

The first words were small. Careful. And then somehow, they were not small anymore. They were 3 a.m. confessions, they were half-jokes that meant everything, they were the kind of silences that feel like being held.

Some people are chapters. You were a whole library. Every conversation opened a new room, and every room had more light in it than the last.

iii.

Learning Each Other by Heart

I learned that your laugh arrives in stages — the small breath, the pause, then the real one that changes the room. I learned how you go quiet when you're tired, and how your voice softens when you're telling the truth.

Every small thing became precious. The way you type. The way you wait for people. The way you worry, beautifully, about things no one else notices.

iv.

The Knowing

There was no single moment when it happened. It was a slow gravity. A thousand small choices to stay, to listen, to remember. And then one day I looked at the sky and realized I had been looking for you in every constellation my whole life — and there you were. Not found. Home.

The Beginning

First Light

A message, a reply, a small spark. Neither of us knew that the sky had just quietly reorganized itself.

The Long Months

The Slow Unfolding

Conversations grew longer. Silences grew softer. I started noticing small things you said, and saving them somewhere deeper than memory.

The Turning Point

The Moment I Knew

You were telling me something ordinary, and I realized I would listen to you describe anything — the weather, a stranger's shirt — and never tire of it.

Now

This, Whatever This Is

Not labels, not grand declarations. Just the simple truth: I choose you, quietly, every single day.

If you ever wonder whether you matter — I want you to remember this. You have been a beginning for me. Not a chapter, not a detour. A beginning. The kind of beginning that makes the rest of the story worth writing.

I don't need you to be perfect. I need you to be you — the version of you that laughs mid-sentence, that overthinks, that cares too much about the wrong people and not enough about yourself. That version. That one is my favourite.

Yours, in every lifetime

✦ i. a quiet revelation ✦

The Sky Learned Your Name

Before I knew you, the stars were only stars — distant, distant, distant, like a language no one spoke. And then you arrived, the way morning arrives, without asking, without knocking, just there, and suddenly every constellation was trying to spell your name. I did not fall in love. I noticed — the way you notice that the room was always warm and you had only just learned what cold was.

✦ ii. what i noticed first ✦

A Small Thing

It was not your face or your voice or anything you said on purpose. It was how you paused before you laughed — that tiny breath of hesitation, as if joy needed to be invited in. I fell in love with that pause. With the moment before you became yourself. Because that is where the truest part of a person hides, and it was hiding right in front of me.
✦
✦ iii. a promise in disguise ✦

If You Forget

If you forget tomorrow, or forget me, Or if the years erase my face and name, Remember this, if nothing else can be — That once, a stranger quietly became A home you didn't know you needed. Yes, And once, some voice said stay, and you said yes, And once, the sky rearranged itself to fit The shape of you, and I was here for it. So if the world forgets to be gentle — know That someone keeps your laughter in a room Where no one steals it, no one lets it go, And every morning, it still finds you. Soon You'll hear it in the way the morning comes, Softly, softly, softly — like it hums.
✦
✦ iv. quietly, about you ✦

Ordinary Magic

You do ordinary things in an extraordinary way. You make tea like it's a ceremony. You text like it's a language only the two of us know. You worry like it's a form of prayer. And I keep thinking — if this is what ordinary looks like when it's next to you, then I have no idea what extraordinary is, and I don't think I want to. Because this — this quiet, small, perfectly imperfect, non-cinematic life of ours — is the best thing I never planned.
✦
✦ v. for the small hours ✦

Night Letter

Tonight the moon is doing that thing where it tries to look smaller than it is — hiding behind clouds, pretending it isn't holding the whole ocean in place. I know that trick. I do it too. Pretend to be less than what I feel for you. But the moon still moves the water. And I still — quietly, stubbornly, beautifully — am moved by you. Sleep well, love. The night has you. And I have the thought of you.
