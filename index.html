RISHIKA

August 23, 2002 — My Soul's Mirror

Before the world taught us names,

Something in the quiet knew us both.

Not by face, not by voice —

but by the shape of our silences.

We are the kind of friends

who do not need to speak to be heard.

A glance across a crowded room,

and the universe holds its breath.

Through every season that tried to pull us apart,

through distances measured in more than miles,

we remained — stubborn, soft, unshakable.

Two threads woven from the same quiet fire.

You are the hand I reach for

when the dark feels too deep to name.

The laughter that finds me

even when I have forgotten how to smile.

If lives were pages in an endless book,

I would find your name in every chapter.

Not as a guest, not as a passing line —

but as the ink itself, the reason the story breathes.

We have been strangers in foreign cities,

siblings in houses made of starlight,

warriors standing back to back against the tide,

and children sharing secrets under the same ancient tree.

In every life, in every form,

our souls recognize each other before our eyes do.

That is why a single word from you

can undo a thousand wounds I never spoke aloud.

You are the mirror that does not lie,

the compass that points to truth when I am lost,

the warmth that stays when every fire has gone cold.

And I? I am simply grateful the universe

chose to place us in the same constellation.

So here is my wish, carried on light:

May every life you live be kind to you.

May you always find the ones who remember you,

the way you remember them.

And may I always be one of them.

Our Unspoken Bond

Some connections are written in the language of synchronicity —

the same fears, the same dreams, the same quiet rebellion against a world that asks us to be less than we are.

We do not merely understand each other. We remember each other.

From a place before time gave us names.

That is why no distance can fade us. No silence can break us.

We are not just friends. We are home to each other.

"In all my lives, in every form the universe takes,
I will find you again —
because souls like ours do not meet by accident."
Forever Yours,
SAYAN 💜
